Why Central Coast Refinishing

Done right the first time — and checked again the next day

Refinishing lives and dies on prep and detail. Here's what sets our work apart.

Industrial-grade coating

A professional spray coating built to last — not an over-the-counter DIY kit that wears off in a season.

Meticulous prep

Every surface is cleaned, repaired and masked with care before a drop of coating goes on. Prep is where a lasting finish is won.

Next-day QC visit

We personally come back the day after to quality-check the finish before the job is called done — something most competitors simply don't do.

The next-day quality-control pass is standard on every job. It's a real point of confidence: you're not left hoping the finish cured right — we confirm it in person before we consider the work finished.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything people usually ask before they call or text.

What can you refinish?

Bathtubs, kitchen sinks, countertops and showers. We also repair chips and cracks, and offer buff & polish for surfaces that just need a refresh rather than a full recoat.

Do I have to replace my tub, sink or countertop?

No. Refinishing resurfaces your existing fixture with a professional, industrial-grade coating so it looks brand new — without demolition or replacement.

How long does the work take?

Most jobs are completed in a single visit. We then return the next day for a hands-on quality-control check before calling the job done.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Call or text a quick description — or a photo — of your project, and we'll give you a free, no-obligation quote.

Are you licensed?

Yes, Central Coast Refinishing is licensed with the City of Watsonville.

What areas do you serve?

Santa Cruz, Monterey and San Benito Counties, with work expanding into the Bay Area.

Can you fix a small chip or crack without a full refinish?

Yes — chip and crack repair is one of our core services, done on its own without recoating the whole fixture.
