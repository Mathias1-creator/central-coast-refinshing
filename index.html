Why Central Coast Refinishing

Done right the first time — and checked again the next day

Refinishing lives and dies on prep and detail. Here's what sets Alex's work apart.

Industrial-grade coating

A professional spray coating built to last — not an over-the-counter DIY kit that wears off in a season.

Meticulous prep

Every surface is cleaned, repaired and masked with care before a drop of coating goes on. Prep is where a lasting finish is won.

Next-day QC visit

Alex personally comes back the day after to quality-check the finish before the job is called done — something most competitors simply don't do.

The next-day quality-control pass is standard on every job. It's a real point of confidence: you're not left hoping the finish cured right — Alex confirms it in person before he considers the work finished.
