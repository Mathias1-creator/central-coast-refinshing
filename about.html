The Story

A family business built on craftsmanship

With 10+ years of hands-on refinishing experience, I started Central Coast Refinishing in 2023 with one goal in mind: to bring honest, high-quality craftsmanship and exceptional customer service to our local community. As a family-owned and operated business, we treat every customer like family and every project like it's our own home.

We specialize in refinishing bathtubs, showers, sinks, countertops, tile and shower pans — giving worn or outdated surfaces a beautiful, like-new finish while saving our customers thousands of dollars compared to replacement. We're licensed with the City of Watsonville and serve Santa Cruz, Monterey and San Benito Counties, with work expanding into the Bay Area.

The Approach

Why the work lasts

Every project is completed with premium materials, careful preparation and an obsessive attention to detail — the industrial-grade coating we apply is built to last, not a store-bought DIY kit. We personally return the day after every job for a hands-on quality-control visit, confirming the finish is exactly right before we call it done.

Our reputation is built on quality, honesty and reliability. From your free estimate to the final walkthrough, our goal is to make the whole process simple, stress-free, and to exceed your expectations. Thank you for trusting our family with your home.
