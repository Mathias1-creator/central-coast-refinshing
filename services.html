Refinishing, repair & restoration

Three focused services that give worn bathtubs, sinks, countertops and showers a brand-new look — professionally, cleanly, and without the cost of replacement. Every job is backed by a next-day quality-control visit.

01 Refinishing

Resurfaced to look brand new

Bathtubs, kitchen sinks, countertops and showers are cleaned, repaired, masked and sprayed with a professional-grade coating that bonds to the existing surface. The result looks and feels like a new fixture — glossy, smooth and durable — without tearing anything out, hauling anything away, or the days of demolition a replacement takes.

  • Worn, stained or dated tubs, sinks and showers
  • Tired countertops you want to modernize
  • Rentals and remodels on a budget and a timeline

02 Chip Repair

Fix the damage, keep the fixture

A chip in the enamel or a hairline crack doesn't mean the whole tub or sink has to go. Alex repairs and blends the damaged area so it disappears into the surrounding surface — restoring a smooth, sealed finish and stopping small damage from turning into a costly replacement.

  • Chips in tubs, sinks and countertops
  • Hairline cracks in showers and vanities
  • Color-matched to your existing fixture

03 Buff & Polish

Bring back the shine

Not every fixture needs a full recoat. When a tub or sink has simply gone dull, Alex buffs and polishes the surface back to a like-new shine — a faster, lighter-touch refresh that makes a tired bathroom or kitchen feel clean and cared-for again.

  • Tubs and sinks that have lost their gloss
  • Surfaces in good shape that just need a refresh
  • A lighter-touch alternative to a full recoat

Our Process

How it works

A simple, three-step sequence — with a quality check built into the end of every job.

1

Call or text for a free quote

Reach out with a quick description or a photo of your tub, sink, countertop or shower. Alex gives you a free, no-pressure quote.

2

Prep & spray the coating

Alex meticulously preps the surface — cleaning, repairing and masking — then sprays the industrial-grade coating for a smooth, durable, like-new finish.

3

Next-day quality check

He returns the following day for a hands-on quality-control pass, confirming the finish is flawless before the job is called done.
